How to reach us

Write to {{org.supportEmail}} from the email address you sign in with. Replies within 2 business days; privacy and data-deletion requests are answered sooner and can also go to {{org.privacyEmail}}.

What to include

  • App version — Profile screen in the app (e.g. {{app.versionName}}).
  • Phone and Android version — e.g. “Xiaomi, Android 16”.
  • What you did, what happened, how often — “started a 40-minute meeting, playback stopped at 12 minutes, happens every time”.
  • Which transcription mode — offline or cloud, and which model if you chose one.
  • A screenshot of any error text.
  • Time of day and rough meeting title, when a server-side problem is suspected — it lets us find the request without reading your content.

Please do not send full transcripts of meetings that contain other people’s personal data unless they are needed to reproduce a bug. A few lines is enough.

Before you write

SymptomCheck first
Recording stops or is cut shortAndroid battery optimisation for {{app.name}} is off; the recording notification is present while recording; enough free storage on the phone.
Sign-in fails (code 10 / ApiException)Update the app from Google Play, then restart the phone. If it persists, tell us the exact message — it usually means the certificate Google checks has changed.
Transcription is slow or failsCloud mode needs a stable upload; large meetings upload in parts. Try a shorter part, or switch that meeting to offline transcription.
A meeting is missing on another deviceRecordings live on the phone that made them. Cloud-transcribed meetings and their minutes sync; the audio file itself may not.
Premium not applied after purchaseWait a minute in the app, then restart it. Still nothing: send us the order number from Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
Want your data goneProfile → Delete account in the app. It removes recordings, transcripts, minutes and the account. No email needed.

For app reviewers

{{app.name}} requires a Google sign-in and has no anonymous or demo mode. If you need an account to review the app, contact {{org.supportEmail}} and we will provide access details, typically within 24 hours. Your reviewers’ account data is treated exactly as described in the Privacy Policy, and is deleted on request.

Reporting something serious

For a security issue, a suspected data exposure, or a request from someone recorded in another person’s meeting, use {{org.privacyEmail}} with the word “security” or “data request” in the subject line. We acknowledge these the same business day where we can.

What we do not do

No phone support, no remote access to your device, and no support by messaging app. We will never ask for your password, a payment PIN, or a full backup of your meetings.
