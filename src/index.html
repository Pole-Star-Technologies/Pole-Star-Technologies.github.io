What it does

Record

One tap starts a recording; the mic indicator stays on your phone the whole time. Long meetings survive the screen turning off, and a paired headset or car kit can be the input.

Transcribe

Two modes: offline, where a Whisper model runs on your phone and your audio is not uploaded, or cloud, where audio goes through our service to Google’s Gemini API for the hard parts.

Minutes

A summary, the key points, action items, who spoke and an overall read of the meeting — written from the transcript, editable afterwards.

Library

Playback, search, share, and per-meeting history with duration and size. Delete a meeting when you are done with it.

Privacy, stated plainly

  • No advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers in the app.
  • Offline transcription means audio that never leaves your phone.
  • Storage is private and row-scoped: your meetings are reachable only by your account.
  • Location is optional, used for a sign-in security record only, never while recording.
  • Profile → Delete account removes your recordings, transcripts, minutes and account, in an order the server enforces so nothing is orphaned.

The full detail — every category of data, every permission and what each is for, who processes what, retention, and your rights — is in the Privacy Policy. It is written against the build that ships, not against a template.
